## Originality boundary

The broad perception--reasoning diagnosis is occupied prior art. Do not claim
the first observation that outcome-only multimodal RL weakly supervises
perception, the first perception/reasoning decomposition, the first
right-answer/wrong-reason finding, or the first perception-aware reward. The
current candidate contribution is the joint use of separately estimated
`c_sel`, `c_fork`, and `c_syn`, natural mediator fork replay, a
synthetic-to-natural transport certificate, crossed risks, and multi-interface
partial identification. `paper/related_work_audit_v2.md` records the bounded
search and must be refreshed before submission.

## Promotion requirements

VLM claims require actual natural mediator records, image-cut fork replay,
crossed risks, checkpoint selection ratios, at least two valid interface types,
simultaneous confidence bands, paired mechanism/style shifts, clean provenance,
and completion of every authenticated large-GPU gate. A generated plan is not
an experiment.
